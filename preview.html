Creative Direction.

We are not a social media agency. We are an independent studio that treats a property's space as its most valuable asset, building a point of view rooted in your brand DNA first and everything else around it. We do not create content faster. We create it smarter.

Founded by Hsiu-Li Yeh, a scientist by training, turned interior designer, with 25+ years designing hotels, restaurants, resorts, wellness spaces and luxury residences.

Her extensive interior design background, including work with OMA/Rem Koolhaas and Jan des Bouvrie, and, before that, a childhood spent in her parents' restaurant, is what Yeh Media brings to every project. The science of how design shapes the energy of a space and the people within it: Neuroaesthetics. This is the thread running through everything below.

01Creative Direction

The strategic layer, positioning, voice and storytelling rooted in your brand DNA, built the way an architectural designer builds a room: shaping the experience first, not just how it looks.

02Content Creation

Photography, film and social content, shot on-site, that translate a space's atmosphere, its materials, light and mood, into imagery guests respond to on instinct.

03Strategy

Content and channel strategy, grounded in neuroaesthetics: the psychology of why an image or video makes a guest stop scrolling, save, or book, instantly.

Yeh Media is a focused team of concept strategists, art directors and content producers working across the hospitality industry. Our international network of photographers, filmmakers and stylists is curated project by project, matched to each property's own story.
